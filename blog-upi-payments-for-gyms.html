UPI Payments for Gyms: How to Stop Checking the Payment Notebook

In almost every one of the 110 gyms I've visited, members already pay by UPI. There's a QR code on the counter, and people pay with GPay, PhonePe or Paytm. Collecting the money isn't the problem.

Keeping track of it is. One gym owner put it to me simply: he didn't want to check his payment notebook ever again. His first question wasn't about features — it was whether a payment would be recorded without someone writing it down.

This guide is about that gap: between a member paying and your records knowing they paid.

How it usually works today

The pattern I saw again and again:

  1. A member scans the QR at the counter and pays.
  2. They show the screenshot, or send it on WhatsApp.
  3. Someone writes it in a notebook — now, later, or not at all.
  4. At the end of the month, the owner tries to match the notebook against bank messages and memory.

It works until it doesn't. In the evening rush nobody writes anything down. Two people share the desk and each thinks the other recorded it. A member pays half now and half later. A screenshot sits in a WhatsApp chat for three weeks. And when a member says "I paid last month", there's no quick way to check.

Why UPI is still the right way to collect

  • Members already prefer it. You don't have to teach anyone anything.
  • It costs you little or nothing. UPI payments to a merchant QR are generally free for the merchant, while card machines typically take a percentage of every payment and often a monthly rental.
  • The money goes straight to your bank account. No middleman holding it.

One practical tip: use a business UPI ID in the gym's name rather than the owner's personal one. When gym money and personal money land in the same account, reconciling the month is twice as hard.

The fix: one record, filled in when the payment happens

The notebook fails because recording a payment is a separate job someone has to remember. The fix is to make recording part of the payment itself. In GymExec there are three ways in:

  • Record it in two taps at the counter. Pick the member, the amount, UPI or cash. Their renewal date moves forward automatically.
  • Let the member tell you. When a member pays from the link in their reminder, they can tap "I've paid — notify my gym". You see the claim, confirm it, and it's recorded — no hunting through WhatsApp for screenshots.
  • Upload the screenshot. On the Elite plan, upload a member's UPI screenshot and the AI reads the amount and reference for you.

However it gets in, it lands in one place: a payment ledger that replaces the notebook. Search by name, filter by month, see the month's total, and export it to Excel for your accountant.

Let reminders bring the payment to you

The best payment is one you didn't have to ask for. GymExec's WhatsApp renewal reminders go out before a member's plan ends and include a link to pay. Members who were going to forget pay from the message instead — and you don't have to send anything yourself.

What about fully automatic recording?

Some owners — especially gyms with more than one branch — have asked us for the next step: a member pays, and the records update themselves with nobody touching anything. That's possible, but it means payments flowing through a payment gateway connected to the gym's own bank account, so the gym's money always goes straight to the gym. We're building it carefully for exactly that reason. Until then, the three ways above remove the notebook without changing how your members pay.

A simple setup that works

  1. One business UPI QR at the counter, in the gym's name.
  2. Record every payment at the moment it happens — or let members claim it with "I've paid".
  3. Close the day: check today's payments in the ledger before you lock up.
  4. Turn on renewal reminders so payments come to you.
  5. Export the month to Excel when your accountant asks.

None of this asks your members to change anything. They keep paying the way they already do. You just stop losing track of it.
